Waybar is the bar that keeps the most needed things at hand: the workspaces, the load, the network, the sound, the notifications and the screen effects. On the PC it shows the state of the graphics card, and on the laptop the battery, the backlight and the tablet mode

Many of the indicators are not there for looks alone: a click opens the launcher, the notifications or the settings of the effects. The bar picks up the current theme by itself
